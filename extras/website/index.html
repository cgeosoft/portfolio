Findings

No claim without a source

A finding must cite fact nodes, and every number in it must exist in a node it cites. One repair turn, then the claim is dropped.

Suggestions

A suggestion must act on a finding of the same run. It then passes the same checks as every other suggestion in the app.

Privacy

Track every holding.
Tell no one.

Stays on your disk

  • Holdings and quantities
  • Cash and account balances
  • Every transaction and its cost
  • Portfolio values and history

Leaves, and only this

  • Ticker symbols to Yahoo Finance for prices, plus Finnhub or FMP if you add a key.
  • Weights, percentages and tickers to the AI model you chose, when you ask. Pick a local model and this data stays on your computer.
  • Briefs to your phone over Gotify or ntfy, if you switch it on. Never money amounts.
  • Update checks to GitHub and this website. On by default, and you can switch it off.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports to PostHog, only if you switch them on. Privacy Notice.

Privacy gate

Code enforces this, not a line in a prompt. Before a prompt leaves, the app reads every number in it. If one equals an amount or quantity in your ledger, the send stops.

Features

What it does

  1. Live quotes, any currency

    Stocks, ETFs and crypto from Yahoo Finance, converted to your base currency.

  2. Broker import

    CSV and Excel from Trade Republic, Trading 212, Scalable Capital, Interactive Brokers, DEGIRO, eToro, Revolut, XTB and Saxo, with a dry-run preview.

  3. An assistant you choose

    Ollama, llama.cpp, LM Studio, any OpenAI-compatible key, or the Claude and Codex CLIs.

  4. Exposure look-through

    Sector, country and the stocks inside your funds, with overlap warnings. On the new Insights tab.

  5. Income calendar

    Dividends received and expected, yields and the next ex-dividend dates.

  6. Markets

    Treasury yields, sectors and the economic calendar in one place.

  7. Daily brief

    A scheduled write-up each morning. Missed one while the app was closed? It runs on the next start.

  8. Indicators

    SMA 50/200, RSI 14 and 52-week range for every position.

  9. Metric modules

    Sandboxed WebAssembly cards. Browse them in the catalog.

  10. MCP server

    Let Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor read your portfolio. Read-only, and percentages only.

  11. PIN and home network

    Lock it with a PIN, then open it from your phone on the same network.

  12. One file, no account

    Everything lives in one database on your disk. Back it up by copying the file.

FAQ

Asked first

What does Guided Analysis need?

A portfolio, a brief and a model that can call tools, or the Claude or Codex CLI. A run takes a few minutes and you can leave the page while it works. Without a model, the tracker, Insights and the metric modules still work.

Does it trade for me?

No. Portfolio never connects to your broker. It suggests changes and you decide. It is a record and a research aid, not financial advice.

What does the model see during an analysis?

Weights, percentages, tickers and public market data. Never amounts, quantities or balances. The app computes those on your computer when it turns a suggestion into shares.

Where is my data?

In one database file on your computer. No servers, no account. Back it up by copying the file.

How do live quotes work without a cloud?

The app asks Yahoo Finance for prices directly from your computer. Only the ticker symbols leave; your holdings and values never do. Finnhub and Financial Modeling Prep work the same way if you add a key.

Which AI does the assistant use?

The one you set up. A local runtime keeps these requests on your computer. A cloud key or a CLI sends requests straight to that provider, never through a server of ours. Chats and briefs carry percentages, weights and tickers, not money amounts.

Can I use it from my phone?

On your home network, yes. Set a PIN in Settings, Privacy & Security, switch on Open on other devices, then open the address in your phone's browser.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is open source under the MIT License, with no subscription and no portfolio size limit.
